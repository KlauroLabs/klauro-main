# Large Fabric Fleets

Fabric targets dozens to hundreds of simultaneous human and agent participants over one codebase.
Every participant publishes an attributed semantic stream. Overlapping paths, symbols, functions,
flows, capabilities, entities, contracts, and intents remain independently visible and active.

Current executable evidence includes a 100-participant mixed loopback proof with five reconciliation
rounds and 500 participant-observations, retaining every claim and semantic stream with zero merge
decisions, surprises, or unattributed changes. Five deployed two-machine runs retained every remote
claim and extension; the sustained Linux run exited cleanly after 179 seconds with loopback claim p95
between 100 and 129 milliseconds.
